Literature Club

Reading between the lines

About the Club

The Monada Dominion Literature Club is a gathering for people who read not to collect opinions, but to meet themselves in the text. It sits alongside the Heuristic LAB as a slower, quieter practice—same appetite for direct, unreferenced experience, applied to books instead of exercises.

We are not building a syllabus or a canon. We pick a book, we read it, and we talk about what actually happened in each of us while reading—not what critics, summaries, or reviews say it's supposed to mean.

How We Meet

  • One Book, One Cycle: The club reads one piece at a time, at a pace that respects the text rather than a deadline.
  • Personal Reading First: Come with your own reaction, not a prepared analysis. Notes, feelings, confusion, and disagreement are all welcome material.
  • Open Table: No prerequisite reading history or literary background is required to join a session.
  • In Own Words: As in the LAB, we describe what we found using our own language before reaching for critics' terms.

Formats

Reading Circles

Small-group sessions discussing a shared chapter or short work.

Open Discussions

Free-form conversation on a theme, passage, or question a book raised.

Author Deep-Dives

A longer session tracing one author's work and its throughline.
