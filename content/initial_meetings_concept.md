And there is a scientific perspective behind all of that. Ideally, creative processes and problem solving, innovation and doing new activity must create new neural connections or stimulate a process of creating new neural connections in the brain. 

Just by visiting these events and participating this process is stimulated. Then the effect can be used in ordinary life and improve the routine - solve problems faster.

The goal is to keep participants at the state of the highest intellectual processes involved into problem solving. Creative problem solving. 

This can be anything. And the most perfect area is simply ourselves. 

Mind and body always seek something. We have desires and goals which we want to achieve, but don’t know how. 

Polya, Einstein, Mendeleev and others, Newton and Archimedes - all mention the same idea of inventing something intuitively. Forget about your problem, sleep or play something and then it can be solved intuitively. There is no formula, no universal solution or method. Yet, all of them did it and invented important things. 

There is a scientific explanation with neocortex, reptile and limbic brains. Stress from thinking and work of the neocortex is passed to other brains that are deeply connected to the body and thus the environment and outer world. That allows subconscious processes to interfere and help problem solving. 

 Here it is possible to assume that exercises, games and practices of monada dominion can be helpful. They are unusual, not common to everyday life and allow to switch activity and attention.
